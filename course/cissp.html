CISSP: learn the map, not the dump

CISSP is the highest certification you said you want. It is a management-breadth exam, not a “type Linux commands” exam. Full Sail will not voucher it. You need about five years of paid security work (four with a degree). Until then you can pass the test and be an Associate of ISC2.

Official: https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp

Do not start CISSP books yet if Security+ is unfinished

CISSP assumes you already know Network+ and Security+ ideas, then asks “what should a manager choose?” Spend this module learning the eight domains so later study is organized.

The eight domains

#DomainIn plain English
1Security and Risk ManagementPolicy, law, ethics, risk, CIA
2Asset SecurityClassify data, handle it, destroy it
3Security Architecture and EngineeringSecure design, crypto, facilities
4Communication and Network SecuritySecure networks (your Network+ plus design)
5Identity and Access Management (IAM)Who gets in, least privilege
6Security Assessment and TestingAudits, tests, reviews
7Security OperationsMonitoring, IR, recovery
8Software Development SecurityBuild software without opening holes

How CISSP differs from Security+

  • Security+: “What is hashing?” CISSP: “Which control is most appropriate given this risk and budget?”
  • Security+: technician. CISSP: advisor to the business.
  • Security+: CompTIA voucher possible. CISSP: you pay ISC2 later.

Your ladder (honest timeline)

  1. Now: this course — math, Network+, Security+.
  2. Full Sail vouchers when those courses complete (A+/Network+ or Security+/CySA+ depending on degree).
  3. Job or internships that count as security work.
  4. CySA+ if you are on the analyst path.
  5. CISSP when you have the years — or Associate of ISC2 after the exam if the years are short.

Mindset drills (CISSP-style)

Practice. A company can buy cameras or train staff against tailgating, but not both this quarter. What does CISSP want you to do first?
Assess risk (likelihood × impact) and business need, then pick the control that reduces the most risk for the cost — not the coolest gadget.
Practice. Why is “because the exam dump said C” a bad CISSP strategy?
ISC2 questions change. They test judgment. Learn CIA, risk, and least privilege so you can reason. Dumps also violate exam policy.

When this module is “done”

You can list the eight domains from memory and explain why CISSP waits until after Security+ and real work. Then go back to math and Security+ — that is the work that makes CISSP possible.
